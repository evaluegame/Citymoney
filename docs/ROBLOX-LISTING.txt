CITYMONEY — COPY-READY ROBLOX LISTING

EXPERIENCE NAME
Citymoney — Build Your City

EXPERIENCE DESCRIPTION
Roll the dice and turn empty lots into your own colorful city!

Play a 2-player or 4-player match, invite your friends, or practice with bots.
Choose your first home level, upgrade your buildings, and set higher rent.
Collect cards, complete city challenges, join festivals and protect your homes.
Use a demolition permit to challenge a rival's property and make your next move.

Your player color marks your homes and avatar. Turns advance automatically,
so you can focus on building your city. The optional Waterfront Club adds
cosmetic house finishes, dice styles, a player aura and a walking trail.

PASS NAME
Waterfront Club — Signature Collection

PASS DESCRIPTION
Unlock Golden Estate, Aurora Residence and Rose Conservatory.
Each collection includes coordinated home decorations, dice colors,
a player aura and a walking trail. One permanent purchase unlocks all three.
Cosmetics only: no game cash, extra rent, cards or changes to dice odds.

PASS ICON TO UPLOAD
Club-pass-icon.png in this docs folder (512 x 512 PNG).

OPTIONAL STARTING PRICE
149 Robux. Choose the actual price in Creator Dashboard.
This text does not configure a sale. The game reads the real Roblox price,
including applicable player-specific prices, after you add your pass ID.

SETUP
First publish the experience. Create this pass under the SAME experience,
enable sales, then paste its numeric Asset ID into Config.clubPassId and
republish. Do not use a developer-product ID or another game's pass.
