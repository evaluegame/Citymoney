CITYMONEY — THE WATERFRONT CLUB

OPEN THE UPDATED GAME
1. Extract Citymoney-Waterfront.zip.
2. Open Roblox Studio on a Windows PC or Mac and sign in.
3. Choose File > Open from File (Ctrl+O / Cmd+O).
4. Open Citymoney/build/Citymoney.rbxlx from the extracted ZIP.
5. Press Play (F5), then Start the neighborhood.

Solo games include City Bot. The game interface appears during Play.
Studio download: https://create.roblox.com/

YOUR FIRST HOME
Roll the dice. When you land on an unowned property, choose Buy cottage.
A real furnished house with your name, garden and mailbox appears on that
lot immediately. Collect both properties in its district to upgrade it.

WALK THROUGH YOUR HOME
Click your house (or open Your homes) and select Walk through your home.
Use normal Roblox walking controls. The front-door prompt uses E to open
and close the door. Upgraded houses have stairs and upstairs bedrooms.
Press B or Back to board to return. Other players keep taking turns.

DURING YOUR TURN
Use Upgrade, Repaint or Sell on a home you own. Repainting is free.
The Close-up button gives you a closer architectural view.
Roll/end-turn automatically returns your avatar to the board.

CAMERA
Use Overview, rotation arrows and + / - to move the board camera.
The desktop header lets you switch between golden hour and night.

WITH FRIENDS
Use Studio's Server & Clients test with 2-6 players. The first connected
player hosts and starts the game. Late arrivals play the next match.

PUBLISHING
Use File > Publish to Roblox in your own account. Set server capacity to 6.
Publishing to Roblox is separate from downloading the Studio place.

If your browser displays XML, save the file with the .rbxlx extension,
or download the ZIP instead. README.md contains full rules and controls.
