CITYMONEY — BUILD YOUR CITY
Thai instructions: START-HERE-TH.txt

OPEN IN ROBLOX STUDIO
1. Extract Citymoney-Waterfront.zip.
2. Install Roblox Studio on Windows or macOS and sign in.
3. Choose File > Open from File (Ctrl+O / Cmd+O).
4. Open Citymoney/build/Citymoney.rbxlx from the extracted ZIP.
5. Press Play (F5). Click PLAY NOW or MENU > Play Together, choose 2 or 4 players,
   then click PRACTICE WITH BOTS. Optional practice rules are under SETTINGS.
Studio: https://create.roblox.com/

BRIGHT CITY VIEW AND INTERACTIVE DICE
The colorful city board has empty lots, a card market, festival stage,
challenge hall and broadcast tower. Play starts in an aligned Top view.
Upright badges show short names and prices; RENT marks an owned home's rent.
Tap a badge for the full name, owner and details. 3D view switches to the
angled city. Fit restores Top view and resets the zoom; + / - zoom in/out.
MENU [M] contains all game menus.
EVENTS, CARDS & GOALS and MY HOMES are direct shortcuts. On phones they
are above the board. The larger turn dock explains your next step.
Unbought lots have no houses; choose a level and buy to build your home.
Tap either large HUD die, the bigger 3D dice, or their tray to roll. R also rolls.
Rounded dice with flat pips show the result; the HUD also shows the total.
The shared dice settle on the server's values before your avatar moves.

AUTOMATIC TURNS
The token moves one space at a time: 0.25 seconds moving, then a 0.13-second
pause at the center of each space. It finishes its path before the result. The
four-second next-turn countdown starts after arrival and resolution.
Next player / Space advances sooner after arrival. Buying and opportunity
choices give you a full 55 seconds AFTER movement. Use cards, upgrades and plaza offers BEFORE rolling.
Menus do not pause the handoff countdown.

CITY PLAZA
The center now has clickable stations instead of decorative houses/trees.
EVENTS or P opens four big choices: Festival, Card Market, Challenges, News.
City Market sells chosen tactical cards for game cash. Select a card and
review its price before confirming. Successful offers return to the board.
The festival rotates every eight turns: rent boost ($80), one added building
level ($110), or a demolition shield ($60), applied to your chosen home.
One plaza purchase per turn, before rolling. Playing a card is separate.
Challenge Hall opens goals. City News shows the festival and recent events.

PREMIUM VISUALS / ROBUX PASS
Open MENU > Style Studio. Preview Golden Estate,
Aurora Residence and Rose Conservatory. One optional cosmetic game pass
unlocks all three, with house details, dice themes, auras and walking trails.
City Original stays free. Pass ownership gives no gameplay advantage.
Studio lets you preview/equip all styles for that test session only.

To sell it: publish the place, create a pass for THIS game in Creator
Dashboard > Monetization > Passes, enable sales and choose your Robux price.
Copy its Asset ID. Stop Play in Studio. In Explorer open ReplicatedStorage >
Citymoney > Config, replace clubPassId = 0 with your numeric pass ID,
then publish again. Until configured, live purchases stay disabled.
Use docs/Club-pass-icon.png as the pass icon. Full step-by-step instructions,
current publishing links and troubleshooting are in docs/PUBLISHING.md.

PLAY ONLINE / INVITE FRIENDS
Publish the updated place using File > Publish (or Publish to Roblox).
In Creator Dashboard, make it available to your intended players. A public
lobby capacity of 12 is recommended; matches admit exactly 2 or 4 humans.
Join the published experience in the Roblox app. Open MENU > Play Together,
choose 2 or 4 players, then click PLAY ONLINE to start the automatic search.
Online matches never add bots.

Invite friends BEFORE queueing. Accepting the native invite brings friends
to your server and party. Guests mark Ready; the leader queues the party.
Parties stay together when matched. Cancel while searching; once connecting,
wait for the reserved-match teleport. Return to lobby after the match,
then invite friends again to form your next party.

STUDIO MULTIPLAYER TEST
Use Server & Clients with 2 or 4 clients. On EACH client choose the same
match size and join the queue. It starts automatically when enough players
are queued. This is a local test. Live invitations and cross-server teleports
must be tested in a published experience using the Roblox app.

BUY / UPGRADE YOUR HOME
Land on an unowned lot and choose L0 Cottage, L1 Townhouse, L2 Garden villa,
or L3 Grand villa. Compare the 3D preview, full price, rent and cash left.
Confirm to build your furnished home on the empty lot. SKIP leaves it empty.
Your roof, borders,
flag, mailbox and avatar ring show your color. Signs show your name.
Select your home > Upgrade options to choose a higher level later.
Only added building levels are charged; you do not need a district pair.

CHOOSE HIGHER RENT
Select your home during your turn > Rent options.
Choose 1.5x, 2x or 3x future rent. Compare the fee and new rent, then confirm.
Fees charge only added tier steps: 2 x build cost per tier step.
The multiplier remains until the home is sold or destroyed.

CARDS & GOALS
Open MENU > Cards & Goals. Choose a card, then its property and any required
opponent. One card per turn; hold up to three copies of each type.
- Demolish: destroy a selected opponent's whole house; lot returns to city.
- Shield: protect your selected home against the next demolition.
- Renovate: add one level to your selected home free (max L3).
- Rent boost: +50% rent on your chosen property for two paid collections.
- Demand rent: choose your property and an opponent; demand 2x rent now,
  capped at $350.
Online always uses full demolition. Practice also offers one-level attacks.

Your goal and progress bar are visible on the main screen. Tap a ready
goal to claim cash and a card directly during your turn. Card rewards use
remaining hand space; if the hand is completely full, you still get cash.
One claim per turn; changing the goal costs $50 once per turn.
City News has 20 automatic events. Every SECOND City News visit opens a
City Opportunity: choose cash or a specific card, free. A full card type
is disabled. If you do not choose within 55 seconds, cash is selected.
Cards go into your hand; play them separately in Cards & Goals.
City Gate, City News, the park and goals also award cards.

WALK THROUGH / CAMERA
Select your home > Walk through your home. Use normal Roblox controls.
E opens your front door. B or Back to board returns. Matches keep moving.
Use Top view, City view, Fit, + / -, and Close-up. MENU also has Day/Night.
