CITYMONEY — OPEN AND PLAY

You need Roblox Studio on a Windows PC or Mac.
Install Studio from https://create.roblox.com/ if you do not have it.

1. If you downloaded Citymoney-Studio.zip, extract the ZIP first.
2. Open Roblox Studio and sign in to your Roblox account.
3. Choose File > Open from File (Ctrl+O on Windows / Cmd+O on Mac).
4. Select Citymoney.rbxlx. In the ZIP it is inside Citymoney/build/.
5. The miniature city should appear in the editor.
6. Click Play in Studio's toolbar, or press F5.
7. Dismiss the quick guide with "Got it — let's play".
8. Click "Start the city" to play alone against City Bot.

The menus, dice, and lobby appear during Play, rather than in the editor.
Use Play so that Studio starts the client and its interface.

YOUR FIRST TURN
Click Roll the dice. Buy the property or click Pass, then End turn.
Collect both properties in a colored district to unlock building.
Click a board tile or one of Your City deeds to inspect it.
Use Build to increase rent. Selling a deed asks you to confirm first.

WITH FRIENDS
Use Studio's Server & Clients test with 2–6 players. The first player
to connect is the host and starts the match.

TO PUBLISH
Use File > Publish to Roblox from your own Studio account. Set the
experience's server capacity to 6. This download is not published for you.

IF THE DOWNLOAD SHOWS TEXT IN YOUR BROWSER
Save the file to your computer, keeping the .rbxlx extension, then open
that saved file in Studio. Or download and extract the ZIP instead.
